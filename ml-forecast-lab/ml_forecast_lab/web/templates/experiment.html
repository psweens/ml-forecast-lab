Model Comparison ?Models are ranked by composite mean rank across CV folds (Demšar 2006). Within each fold, models are ranked independently by MAE, RMSE, and MASE; those per-metric ranks are averaged to give a composite fold rank, then averaged across folds. This rewards models that are consistently strong across all error measures.
