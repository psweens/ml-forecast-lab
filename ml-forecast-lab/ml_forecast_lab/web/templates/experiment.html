Cumulative source ?Turn on if the sensor reports a running total (e.g. sensor.energy_today, sensor.demand_today) rather than an instantaneous value. The add-on diffs consecutive readings to recover per-interval consumption before training. Leave off for instantaneous sensors like power, temperature, or humidity.
Daily reset ?Only relevant when Cumulative source is on. Turn on if the counter resets at local midnight (typical for "today" sensors like sensor.energy_today) so the add-on knows to handle the rollover instead of treating it as a huge negative jump.
Non-negative target ?Turn on when the target is physically non-negative (PV power, solar irradiance, household demand, weather rainfall) but isn't a cumulative sensor. Neural models then default to a ReLU output activation (clamps predictions at 0 with no bias) and a small daily-loss-weight (penalises systematic under/over-shoot of the day's total). Without this flag, models with a flat-collapsed forecast on PV-style targets see noticeable shape improvement after retrain. Leave off for signed targets like net grid flow or temperature deltas.

Data & Forecast

Log transform ?Apply log1p to the target before training (and the inverse on predictions). Useful when the target is right-skewed (rainfall, occupancy counts, intermittent EV draw) — the loss then weights small and large values more evenly. Leave off for symmetric or already-Gaussian signals (temperature, baseline power).

Model Comparison ?Two complementary views: Per-Interval Accuracy measures the next-step (h=1) prediction error — what the model gets right at the 30-minute level. Daily Cumulative Accuracy (table below) sums each day's predictions and actuals before computing errors — better for use cases like daily energy or hot-water demand where you care about totals rather than exact timing. Both rankings use a composite mean rank over MAE, RMSE, and MASE — the Demšar-style averaging step, not the full Demšar (2006) Friedman/Nemenyi test (which assumes independent datasets, not CV folds of one series; see docs/RANKING_NOTES.md). 95% bootstrap CIs over fold resamples express the within-experiment noise — when two models' CIs overlap the leaderboard is genuinely "tied". The Per-Interval rank still drives Promote, Tuning, and live forecasting; the Daily rank is informational.

Hyperparameter Tuning ?Bayesian optimisation (Optuna TPE) automatically searches for the best hyperparameters. Each trial trains the model with a single CV fold and reports a composite score that averages MAE, RMSE, and MASE — each normalised against a baseline run with the model's default parameters. A composite of 1.0 matches the default; lower is better (e.g. 0.85 means a 15% average improvement across all three metrics). For neural backends the tuning budget is capped at 40 epochs per trial (with early stopping at patience 8) — enough to rank hyperparameter sets without wasting compute. The final production retrain after "Apply" uses the model's full epoch budget. After tuning, click "Apply Tuned Params, Promote & Retrain" to save the winning params and kick off a full production retrain.

Uncertainty bands ?Empirical coverage: of all the actuals that have arrived, how many fell inside the published 80% confidence band (the `_lower_80` / `_upper_80` companion sensors).
