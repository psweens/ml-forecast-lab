Daily cumulative loss (neural) ?Adds a loss term that penalises error in the predicted cumulative curve at every horizon step. Unlike the per-interval loss (which only cares about local values and tends to regress to the mean on sparse targets), this constrains the SHAPE of the cumulative trajectory — so predictions have to match the actual cumulative curve as it accumulates through the day. Designed for cumulative-origin targets like sensor.x_today (daily demand, daily energy), where users evaluate on the cumulative curve rather than per-interval values. Applies to torch neural models (LSTM, CNN, N-BEATS, N-HiTS, TiDE, DLinear, TSMixer, PatchTST, iTransformer, Crossformer, TimesNet, SparseTSF); silently ignored by tree models (LightGBM, XGBoost).

Solar Physics

Sun elevation ?Degrees above horizon; negative at night. Deterministic from lat/lon — a strong signal for diurnal patterns (solar PV, daytime load).
Clear-sky irradiance ?Theoretical maximum solar GHI (W/m²) under perfect clear-sky conditions via pvlib Ineichen model. Zero at night, peak at solar noon — ideal for solar PV forecasting.

Model Comparison ?Two complementary views: Per-Interval Accuracy measures the next-step (h=1) prediction error — what the model gets right at the 30-minute level. Daily Cumulative Accuracy (table below) sums each day's predictions and actuals before computing errors — better for use cases like daily energy or hot-water demand where you care about totals rather than exact timing. Both rankings use the Demšar (2006) composite over MAE, RMSE, and MASE. The Per-Interval rank still drives Promote, Tuning, and live forecasting; the Daily rank is informational so you can pick the model that matches your objective.

Hyperparameter Tuning ?Bayesian optimisation (Optuna TPE) automatically searches for the best hyperparameters. Each trial trains the model with a single CV fold and reports a composite score that averages MAE, RMSE, and MASE — each normalised against a baseline run with the model's default parameters. A composite of 1.0 matches the default; lower is better (e.g. 0.85 means a 15% average improvement across all three metrics). For neural backends the tuning budget is capped at 40 epochs per trial (with early stopping at patience 8) — enough to rank hyperparameter sets without wasting compute. The final production retrain after "Apply" uses the model's full epoch budget. After tuning, click "Apply Tuned Params, Promote & Retrain" to save the winning params and kick off a full production retrain.
