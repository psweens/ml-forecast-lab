Daily Cumulative Accuracy (table below) sums each day's predictions and actuals before computing errors — better for use cases like daily energy or hot-water demand where you care about totals rather than exact timing. The Per-Interval rank still drives Promote, Tuning, and live forecasting; the Daily rank is informational so you can pick the model that matches your objective.

Hyperparameter Tuning ?Bayesian optimisation (Optuna TPE) automatically searches for the best hyperparameters. Each trial trains the model with a single CV fold and reports a composite score that averages MAE, RMSE, and MASE — each normalised against a baseline run with the model's default parameters. A composite of 1.0 matches the default; lower is better (e.g. 0.85 means a 15% average improvement across all three metrics). For neural backends the tuning budget is capped at 40 epochs per trial (with early stopping at patience 8) — enough to rank hyperparameter sets without wasting compute. The final production retrain after "Apply" uses the model's full epoch budget. After tuning, click "Apply Tuned Params, Promote & Retrain" to save the winning params and kick off a full production retrain.

Uncertainty bands ?Empirical coverage: of all the actuals that have arrived, how many fell inside the published 80% confidence band (the `_lower_80` / `_upper_80` companion sensors). A well-calibrated model should hit ≈ 80% — much higher and the bands are unnecessarily wide; much lower and they're claiming more confidence than the model actually has. Use this to sanity-check whether downstream automations can trust the band.

Run-to-run swing ?For each future moment, we collect the predictions every forecast cycle made of it before it arrived, then measure the typical spread between those predictions. Low values mean the model is confident — successive runs converge on similar values. High values mean predictions for the same moment swing significantly between runs, which makes downstream automations harder to trust. Reported as a percentage of the predicted value.
